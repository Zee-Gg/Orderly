# Rider — quick guide

*(Roman Urdu notes in italics)*

## First time
1. Open the link the manager gives you: **/rider/login** on your phone.
2. Type your **phone number** and **6-digit PIN**. *Apna number aur 6 number wala PIN daalein.*
3. In Chrome: menu (⋮) → **Add to Home screen**. Next time just tap the icon.
4. Forgot your PIN? Ask the manager to set a new one. 5 wrong PINs = wait 15 minutes.

## Every shift
1. Open the app and switch yourself **Online**. *Duty par ho to Online karein.*
2. Keep the phone's sound on. A new delivery **rings and vibrates**. The screen stays on while the app is open.
3. Going off duty? Switch to **Offline**.

## A delivery
1. A new order appears with the customer's name, address, landmark, map button and the amount to collect.
2. Pick up the food → tap **On the way**. The customer gets a WhatsApp message. *Khana le kar "On the way" dabayein.*
3. Tap **Call** if you can't find the address, or **Map** for directions.
4. At the door: take the cash → tap **Delivered** → enter the cash you received. *Jitne paise liye, wohi likhein. Kam ya zyada hon to wajah likhein.*
5. Couldn't deliver? Tap **Failed** and choose why (refused, not reachable, wrong address). Bring the food back.

## Cash
- Your cash total for the shift is on your screen. Give it to the manager at the end of the shift; they mark it settled. *Shift ke end par saare paise manager ko dein.*

## Problems
- No new orders coming? Check you are **Online** and have internet.
- Logged out? Log in again with phone + PIN.
