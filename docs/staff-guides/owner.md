# Owner — quick guide

*(Roman Urdu notes in italics)*

## First login
1. Log in with the email and temporary password we sent you. You will be asked to choose your own password. *Pehli dafa apna naya password banayein.*
2. The orange box at the top shows **Finish setting up**. Open it and finish every step. Each step ticks itself.

## Every day
- **Orders**: new orders ring. Top bar: orders done today, sales today, pending.
- **Inbox**: WhatsApp chats. Red **Needs staff** = the bot asked for help. *Laal wali chats pehle dekhein.*
- **Kitchen** and **Riders** run on their own screens and phones.
- **Open / Closed** switch: when closed, the bot tells customers you're closed and the menu page shows it.

## Your menu = your prices
- Change items, sizes, extras, prices and photos in **Menu**. The bot and the menu page use them right away. *Qeemat sirf Menu se badlein — bot khud se qeemat nahi banata.*
- Something ran out? Mark it **Out of stock**.

## Staff
- **Settings → Staff**: add managers, cashiers, kitchen. One login per person. Switch someone off when they leave.
- **Riders** page: add riders with a PIN, reset PINs, settle their cash.

## Reports
- **Reports**: sales, best sellers, busy hours, riders, customers, the bot. Pick Today / This week / This month. Download CSV files.

## Billing
- **Settings → Billing**: your plan, when to pay, how to pay, and receipts.
- Banners warn you when the trial ends or payment is due. If payment is late after the grace days, online ordering stops until you pay — nothing is deleted. *Waqt par payment karein taa ke orders band na hon.*

## Data and privacy
- **Settings → Data & privacy**: download all orders, customers and menu as CSV.
- A customer asks to delete their data? Search their phone there, type DELETE, confirm. Their orders stay in your totals as "Deleted customer".

## Need help?
- WhatsApp us (the number is on the Contact page). Tell us the order number and what happened.
