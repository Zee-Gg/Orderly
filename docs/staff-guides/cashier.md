# Cashier / Order taker — quick guide

*(Roman Urdu notes in italics — Urdu mein madad ke liye)*

## Start of shift
1. Open the dashboard on the counter computer or phone and **log in**. *Apna email aur password daalein.*
2. Check the branch name at the top. If you work at one branch it's chosen for you.
3. Turn the **sound ON** (speaker button). New orders ring. *Awaaz on rakhein — naya order aane par ghanti bajegi.*
4. If the restaurant is open, the **Open** switch should be green.

## New orders (Orders page)
- New orders appear in **New** (amber) with a sound. WhatsApp orders from the AI show an **AI** tag.
- Tap the order → check items, address and phone → **Accept**. *Order check karein, phir Accept dabayein.*
- Can't make it? **Reject** and choose a reason. The customer gets a polite message.
- Colours: New = amber · Accepted = blue · Preparing = orange · Ready = green · On the way = purple · Delivered = grey · Cancelled = red.

## Phone or walk-in order
1. **New order** → choose items, size and extras. *Prices khud lag jati hain — kabhi hath se mat likhein.*
2. Delivery: type the phone (old customers fill in by themselves), pick the **area** (fee fills in).
3. Choose payment → **Place order**.

## Delivery
- When food is ready, tap **Assign rider** and pick a free rider (online riders are at the top).
- The rider marks **On the way** and **Delivered** from their phone.

## Printing
- **Print bill** from the order. *Bill print karne ke liye order kholein aur Print bill dabayein.*

## WhatsApp Inbox
- **Needs staff** (red) = the bot needs help. Open it, read the chat, reply.
- When you type a reply, the bot stops for that chat. When done, tap **Hand back** or **Resolve**. *Kaam khatam ho to "Resolve" dabayein — bot dobara jawab dega.*
- Type **/** to use quick replies (like /time).
- You can only reply within 24 hours of the customer's last message.

## Problems
- "Already updated by someone else" → the screen refreshes; check again.
- No sound? Tap the speaker button once.
- Internet slow? A yellow bar shows "checking every 20 seconds" — orders still come.
- Ask the manager before cancelling an order that is already **Preparing**.
