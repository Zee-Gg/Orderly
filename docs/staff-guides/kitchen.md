# Kitchen — quick guide

*(Roman Urdu notes in italics)*

## Start of shift
1. On the kitchen tablet open the **Kitchen** app (home screen icon) and log in with the kitchen account. *Kitchen account se login karein.*
2. Keep the tablet **plugged in**. The screen stays on by itself.
3. Tap the screen once so the sound can play. *Ek dafa screen ko touch karein taa ke awaaz aaye.*

## Cooking an order
1. A new ticket appears when the counter **accepts** an order. It shows the order number, items, sizes, extras, "without" items and notes. *Kya banana hai — sab ticket par likha hai.*
2. Tap **START** when you begin cooking. The ticket turns orange.
3. Tap **READY** when it's packed. The counter and the rider are told. *Tayyar ho jaye to READY dabayein.*

## Read carefully
- **Without onion / no ice** etc. are shown in red. *"Without" wali cheez zaroor dekhein.*
- **CHANGED** = the order was edited after you saw it. Read it again and tap OK.
- **CANCELLED** = stop cooking. Tap OK to clear it.
- A ticket turns **red** when it's late.

## Printing a kitchen slip (KOT)
- Tap the print button on the ticket. The slip has no prices or phone numbers.

## Out of stock
- If an item runs out, tell the manager or mark it **Out of stock** in the menu. The bot will stop selling it. *Koi cheez khatam ho jaye to foran batayein.*

## Light / dark screen
- Use the theme button for a dark screen at night. It is saved on this tablet only.
